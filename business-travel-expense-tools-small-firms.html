Best Travel & Expense Tools for Small Firms: Navan vs Perk vs Expensify

If two or more people at your firm travel for clients, court or conferences, someone is losing hours to receipts and reimbursements. Here is what the three tools small firms ask about most actually cost, and which one fits how often you travel.

UPDATED OCT 2026 9 MIN READ CATEGORY: TRAVEL & EXPENSE
The short answer For a 2–20 person firm with regular team travel, Navan is the strongest starting point: travel booking costs nothing on its business plan and expense tracking is free for the first five active users. Perk (formerly TravelPerk) makes sense if you want a free starter plan and accept a booking fee on every trip. Expensify fits firms that mostly need receipts and reimbursements, not a booking tool. If you travel less than once a month, a spreadsheet is still fine.

Navan: best for firms that travel every month

Navan puts flights, hotels and cars in the same app as expense reports. When someone books a trip, the cost is already on the expense side, so nobody photographs receipts or rebuilds a report at month end. For small companies, travel booking on the business plan carries no subscription or booking fee; Navan earns from travel suppliers instead. Expense tracking is free for the first five people who submit expenses in a month, then roughly $15 per active user.

That pricing is why it suits a small firm: a five-person practice can run travel and expenses without a monthly bill. Where it is weaker is at very low volume. If the firm travels twice a year, learning a new tool costs more time than it saves. Larger companies sign custom contracts, but that is outside what most small firms will need.

We looked at Navan in more detail in our full Navan review. For law firms, see also how small law firms track client travel expenses.

Perk (formerly TravelPerk): a free plan with a fee on every trip

Perk's Starter plan costs nothing per month, but each booking carries a 5% fee. Premium ($99/month) and Pro ($299/month) lower that fee to 3% and add support and policy features. A spend add-on covers expenses and cards for roughly $11–15 per user per month.

The math is simple: on $2,000 of monthly travel, the Starter fee is about $100 a month, which is close to what Premium costs. Perk is a reasonable choice for a firm that books a handful of trips a year and wants a polished booking tool without a commitment. For regular team travel, the per-booking fee adds up faster than Navan's model.

Expensify: best when you only need expenses

Expensify started as a receipt and reimbursement tool and is still strongest there. Its free tier covers basic receipt scanning, and paid plans start around $5 per user per month, with lower effective prices if the firm uses the Expensify Card. It now offers travel booking inside the app, but booking is not its core strength and booking fees are not clearly published.

Choose it if your people book travel themselves and your real pain is chasing receipts and getting reports to your bookkeeper. If you want travel and expenses handled together, Navan or Perk will do more of the work.

Which one fits your firm

Quick decision guide
  • 2–20 people, someone travels most months: start with Navan.
  • Occasional trips, want zero monthly cost: Perk Starter, and watch the 5% fee as travel grows.
  • Travel booked by each person, receipts are the problem: Expensify.
  • Under one trip a month: keep your spreadsheet and revisit when travel picks up.

Frequently asked questions

Is Navan really free for a small firm?

Travel booking on Navan's business plan has no subscription or booking fee for smaller companies, and expense tracking is free for the first five monthly active users. Above that, expense users cost roughly $15 each per month. Confirm the current terms with Navan, because pricing can change.

What happened to TravelPerk?

TravelPerk now operates under the name Perk. The plans and booking-fee model described above are Perk's current offering.

Can these tools track travel by client or matter?

All three let you tag expenses with custom fields or categories, which is how most firms map costs to a client or matter. Check the field options during a trial before you commit.

Do I need a company card?

No, but it helps. Each tool works with reimbursements, and each offers its own card that makes matching receipts automatic.
