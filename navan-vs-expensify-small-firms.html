Navan vs Expensify for Small Firms: Travel-First or Receipts-First?

Navan is a corporate travel platform that handles expenses in the same app. Expensify is an expense and receipt tool that now books travel too. For a 1–20 person law, accounting or consulting firm, the right pick depends on whether your real problem is booking trips or chasing receipts, and on how many people submit expenses each month.

UPDATED NOV 2026 8 MIN READ CATEGORY: TRAVEL & EXPENSE
The short answer Pick Navan if people at your firm travel to clients, court or audits most months: travel booking carries no fee for companies with 300 or fewer employees, and expenses are free for the first five people who submit them each month. Pick Expensify if travel is occasional and your real pain is receipts, mileage and reimbursements: its Collect plan is $5 per member per month and includes QuickBooks Online and Xero integrations, but each trip booked through Expensify Travel carries a $15 fee.

Navan: travel first, expenses included

Navan started as a corporate travel booking tool, and travel is still the center of the product. Flights, hotels and cars are booked in the same app where expenses are reported, so a booked trip is already on the expense side without anyone forwarding a confirmation email or rebuilding a report at month end.

The pricing is what makes it work for a small firm. According to Navan's pricing page, travel is free for companies with 300 or fewer employees, with no limit on how many trips you book. Expense management is free for the first five monthly expensing users and $15 per user per month after that. A user only counts in a month in which they actually submit an expense, so a twelve-person firm where four people travel regularly would usually stay inside the free allowance. Note that "free up to 300 employees" applies to travel only; expenses follow the five-user rule.

The second practical point is cards. Navan says Navan Connect lets companies enroll existing corporate cards from more than 250 banks, including Visa, Mastercard and American Express, so transactions flow into Navan Expense while the firm keeps its current card rewards and bank relationship. Navan notes that Visa and Mastercard transactions report in real time and American Express transactions daily, and not every Amex program is eligible, so check your specific card before planning around it.

Expensify: receipts first, travel added

Expensify built its name on receipt scanning and reimbursement, and that is still where it is strongest. The current pricing, per Expensify's help center, has two business plans:

  • Collect costs $5 per unique member per month, billed pay-per-use. It is aimed at small teams and includes the QuickBooks Online and Xero integrations, which covers most small law, accounting and consulting firms. Organizations whose first workspace was created before April 1, 2025 may be on legacy Collect billing, which counts active members instead.
  • Control costs $36 per active member per month on pay-per-use. Only Control can be put on an annual subscription, and using the Expensify Card lowers the per-member price further. Control adds multi-step approval workflows, advanced policy controls and integrations such as NetSuite and Sage Intacct, which a 1–20 person firm rarely needs.

Travel booking is available through Expensify Travel. According to Expensify's help documentation, it charges a single $15 fee per trip for self-service bookings, regardless of how many bookings are on the trip, and $25 when a travel agent books or manages the trip for you. That fee is modest for a firm that books a few trips a year, but it adds up: twenty trips a year is $300 in trip fees before any per-user cost.

What it costs a real small firm

Worked example — 8-person firm, 4 regular travelers, 3 trips a month

Navan: travel booking $0. Four people submitting expenses each month fall inside the five free expensing users, so $0 a month. If a sixth person starts submitting expenses, that month costs $15.

Expensify Collect: 8 members × $5 = $40 a month for expenses if every member is on the workspace, plus 3 trips × $15 = $45 in Expensify Travel fees, about $85 a month. If you only add the four travelers to the workspace, the per-member part drops to $20, and if people book travel elsewhere, the trip fees disappear.

The numbers turn the other way for a firm with more expense submitters and little travel. A 15-person accounting practice where everyone submits mileage and receipts but almost nobody flies would pay Navan $15 × 10 = $150 a month for expensing users beyond the free five, against $75 a month on Expensify Collect.

Which one fits your firm

Quick decision guide
  • Someone travels to clients, court or audits most months: Navan. Free booking and no per-trip fee.
  • Five or fewer people submit expenses each month: Navan costs nothing for both travel and expenses.
  • Many people submit receipts or mileage, but few travel: Expensify Collect at $5 per member is likely cheaper.
  • You want to keep your current corporate cards: Navan Connect is built for that; check your card program is eligible.
  • Your bookkeeper lives in QuickBooks Online or Xero and you just want clean reports: Expensify Collect covers both integrations.
  • Fewer than one trip a month and only a handful of receipts: neither yet. A spreadsheet is still fine.

Frequently asked questions

Is Navan or Expensify cheaper for a small firm?

It depends on how many people submit expenses and how often you travel. Navan charges nothing for travel and nothing for up to five monthly expensing users, then $15 per user. Expensify Collect charges $5 per member per month, plus $15 per trip if you book through Expensify Travel. Travel-heavy firms with few submitters usually pay less on Navan; firms with many submitters and little travel usually pay less on Expensify.

Does Expensify charge for travel booking?

Yes. Expensify's help center lists a $15 fee per trip for self-service bookings and $25 when a travel agent books or manages the trip. Navan does not charge booking fees for companies with 300 or fewer employees.

Which plan does a small firm need on Expensify?

Most 1–20 person firms will fit Collect, which includes QuickBooks Online and Xero. Control is built for multi-level approvals and larger accounting systems like NetSuite and Sage Intacct.

Can I keep my current corporate card with either tool?

Navan Connect is designed for that and supports eligible Visa, Mastercard and American Express programs. Expensify is built to pair with its own Expensify Card, which also lowers the Control plan price.

Can either tool track travel by client or matter?

Both let you categorize spend so it can be mapped to a client or matter. For law firms, see our guide on how small law firms track client travel expenses, and check the field options during a trial.
